Sequential 6-Stage Master Script Flow
Stage 1: Opening Contact Verification
"Hi, good morning — am I speaking with Rahul?"
Waits for confirmation before revealing pitch. Respects wrong numbers immediately.
Stage 2: Intro & Hook Advisory Check
"Hi, I’m Dylan Dsouza, a property advisor. I just wanted to check if you are exploring properties in Bangalore?"
Low-pressure intro establishing identity and qualifying current market interest.
Stage 3: Permission Requirement Gathering
"I just need to ask a few quick questions to better understand your requirements — would that be alright?"
Secures buyer consent before diving into the 5-point qualification framework.
Stage 4: Q1 Config 2 BHK vs 3 BHK
"Are you looking for a 2 or 3 BHK?"
Immediate configuration filter. Skipped dynamically if already volunteered.
Stage 4: Q2–Q5 Framework Size, Location, Purpose, Budget
"Any particular size in mind? ... And which location do you prefer? ... Is it for self-use or investment? ... May I know your budget?"
Spoken one by one with conversational active listening markers ("Got it!", "Understood").
Stage 5: Pitch Mittal Palms Walkthrough
"Mittal Palms is spread across 3.2 acres in Jakkur, offering ready-to-move 2 and 3 BHK residences with a clubhouse and landscaped gardens."
Spoken walkthrough: 3.2 Acres, S+G+7 Floors, 2 & 3 BHK ready-to-move-in with OC, clubhouse, pool & gardens in Jakkur.
Stage 6: Close WhatsApp & Callback
"I’ll share the complete details with you on WhatsApp so you can take a look at the floor plans. Could we connect tomorrow to discuss this further? Please let me know a suitable time for you."
Delivers instant floor plans via WhatsApp and locks down a scheduled next-day callback.
Stage 6: Sign-off Courteous Goodbye
"Thank you for your time. Have an amazing day. Goodbye!"
Polite and warm sign-off leaving a premium brand impression.
12-Point Objection & Rejection Handling Matrix
Caller is Busy / Meeting Instant Respect
Caller: "I'm in a meeting right now / driving."
"Completely understand! When would be a convenient time for me to give you a quick callback later today?"
Send on WhatsApp Only Agree + 1 Micro Question
Caller: "Don't call me, just WhatsApp brochure."
"Absolutely! I will share the brochure on WhatsApp right away. Just to send the right floor plan, are you considering a 2 or 3 BHK?"
Budget Out of Range Validate & Pivot
Caller: "That's way too expensive for me."
"Understood! We also have flexible layouts and upcoming phases with convenient payment plans. What budget range were you ideally targeting?"
Not Interested Right Now Horizon Inquiry
Caller: "I'm not interested in buying right now."
"Understood, thank you for letting me know! Are you planning to explore something in the next 6 to 12 months, or should I take this off for now?"
Where Did You Get Number? Full Transparency
Caller: "How did you get my phone number?"
"You had enquired regarding residential properties in Bangalore through an online portal. If this was by mistake, I can immediately update our records."
Location Mismatch Mittal Palms Exclusive Focus
Caller: "I only want Whitefield or Sarjapur."
"Mittal Palms is located in Jakkur, North Bangalore, very close to Manyata Tech Park and Hebbal. At the moment, this is our exclusive project. Would that North Bangalore commute work for you?"
Ready to Move vs Under Construction OC Status Clarity
Caller: "I only want ready with OC, no risk."
"That is smart. This project is 100% ready-to-move with full Occupancy Certificate in hand, so there is zero construction risk."
RERA & Bank Approvals Tier-1 Bank Backing
Caller: "Is it RERA approved? Which banks fund it?"
"Yes, fully RERA approved with clear titles. Leading banks including SBI, HDFC, and ICICI have already approved up to 80% funding."
Plots / Villas Only Filter & Referral
Caller: "I only want an independent villa or plot."
"Got it! While this specific community offers luxury high-rise apartments, I can check our villa options for you. Would that be helpful?"
Already Booked Property Congratulate & Close
Caller: "I already bought an apartment last month."
"Congratulations on your new home! Thank you so much for your time, and I wish you all the best."
Discount / Negotiation Invite for On-Site Spot Offer
Caller: "Is the price negotiable? What discount can you give?"
"Our base pricing is standardized, but our relationship manager can offer unit-specific spot incentives during your site visit."
Hard Rejection / Do Not Call Immediate DNC Compliance
Caller: "Remove my number immediately. Don't call again."
"I truly apologize for any disturbance. I will mark your number on our Do Not Call list right away. Have a good day."
