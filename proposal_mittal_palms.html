1. Core Capabilities: Dual-Bucket Prospecting & Self-Learning

✓ Good Prospects Bucket (Immediate Action)
  • Verified Budget: Aligned with 2 BHK (₹1.15 Cr+) or 3 BHK (₹1.70 Cr+).
  • Immediate Possession: Seeking ready-to-move homes in Jakkur.
  • Walkthrough Scheduled: Locked in weekend site visit with Raghavendra.
  • Instant Alert: Direct WhatsApp & CRM ping to sales closers.
⚠️ Bad Prospects Bucket (Auto-Filtered)
  • Budget Mismatch: Inquiries seeking 3 BHK under ₹50 Lakhs.
  • Rental & PG Hunters: Looking for leases rather than property purchase.
  • Unserviceable Corridors: Refusing North Bangalore commute.
  • Saves 200+ Sales Hours: Polite handling without wasting sales rep time.
🧠 Continuous Post-Call Self-Training Flywheel (Train-After-Every-Call)
  • Autonomous Transcript Analysis: The moment each phone call concludes, the LLM analyzes buyer questions, sentiment, objections, and negotiation attempts.
  • Dynamic Learning Database: New objections (e.g. Kaveri water, airport transit time, payment milestones) are committed into the active intelligence memory in real time.
  • Few-Shot In-Context Injection: By the very next phone call, Priya automatically adopts the optimal answers learned from previous calls—making the agent progressively sharper and more persuasive every single day.

  • Simran (Conversational Sales Persona): Energetic, warm, and articulate Indian English tone.
  • Native Real Estate Phonetics: Flawless pronunciation of local Bangalore terminology (Jakkur, Hebbal, Manyata Tech Park, Airport Road, Crores, Lakhs, 2 BHK, 3 BHK).
  • Hardware Barge-In Support: Sub-50ms clear buffer flush allowing callers to interrupt naturally.
